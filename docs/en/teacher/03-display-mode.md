# What to show, the start date and the description

The **What to show** field:

- **Broadcast, then recording** (default) — while the broadcast runs the
  learner sees the broadcast; once it is over and a recording exists, the
  recording;
- **Broadcast only** — the learner never sees the recording;
- **Recording only** — the learner never sees the broadcast, even while it
  runs: until the broadcast is over — including while the signal is lost and the
  broadcast may resume — the page says the recording will appear
  after it, and neither the recording of the running broadcast nor a "watch the
  recording" link gets around that.

The **Starts at** field is optional. The date you set appears on the course
card, in the course calendar and in the learner timeline.

The **Description** field is the ordinary Moodle one: write what the session is
about. The "Display description on course page" checkbox puts the text onto the
course page, under the activity name.
