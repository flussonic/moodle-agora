Under the player there are the ordinary Moodle comments bound to the activity:
the new comment field on top, the feed below it, adding and deleting without a
page reload. Comments are not tied to a moment in the video: this is not a
chat.

![The same comment through the teacher's eyes](../../img/guide/06-teacher-comments.png)

**Who may write.** A learner with the "Comment on an Agora broadcast"
capability — granted by default to the student, the teacher and the manager. On
top of that, site comments and the plugin setting
["Comments under the player"](../admin/06-settings.md#comments-under-the-player)
must be on; both belong to the Moodle administrator. If any of this is missing,
the learner sees the feed but not the new comment field, and with the plugin
setting off there is no comment area at all.

**How to delete.** You delete someone else's comment with the bin icon in the
comment row. That is the standard Moodle "delete comments" capability.

**Moderation.** The administrator sees every comment of the site in the **Site
administration → Reports → Comments** report. Comments cannot be switched off
for a single stream — the switch is site-wide. Comments are included in the
course backup and restored together with the activity.
