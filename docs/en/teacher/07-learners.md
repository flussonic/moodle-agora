Enrol as usual — with any Moodle enrolment method. Manual enrolment:
**Participants → Enrol users**, or **Participants → Enrolment methods → Manual
enrolments**.

![The manual enrolment form: a role and a person from the "not enrolled" list](../../img/guide/06-enrol-form.png)

The role is chosen **before** pressing Add: the enrolment goes out with the
role selected at that moment. Someone enrolled without a role stays in the
participant list with no rights and cannot watch.

Check the result on the participants page rather than by the fact that you
pressed the button: everyone must have a role.

![The participants page: a teacher and two learners with their roles](../../img/guide/06-participants.png)

**Groups** work as usual. A "group B only" restriction is the ordinary Moodle
access restriction on the activity — the plugin honours it and has no setting
of its own for it. Groups are created as usual: **Participants → Groups**.

So the right to watch equals membership in the course: the learner is enrolled,
the activity is available to them, and the access restrictions are satisfied.
