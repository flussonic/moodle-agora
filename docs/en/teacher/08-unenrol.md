# Unenrolment and what happens to the ticket

For: the teacher.

---

Keeping someone off the page is not enough — the ticket already issued has to
be taken back. The plugin revokes a person's live tickets for the streams of
the course on five Moodle events:

- unenrolment from the course;
- suspension of the enrolment;
- removal from a group;
- a role removed, after which the activity became unavailable;
- deletion of the user.

The Agora server closes a running viewing within seconds — not at the end of
the session, but at once.

To unenrol: **Participants** → the bin icon in the person's row, or the same
manual enrolment page.

![Unenrolling a learner](../../img/guide/07-unenrol-form.png)

![The participants page after the unenrolment](../../img/guide/07-participants-after.png)

An unenrolled person no longer reaches the activity page: the link leads to the
enrolment page with a refusal and they never see the player.

![An unenrolled learner does not get into the course](../../img/guide/07-student-denied.png)

What to keep in mind:

- **Suspending the enrolment** revokes tickets just like unenrolling.
- **Editing the enrolment dates** is not a reason to revoke: the enrolment is
  still active.
- **An enrolment that ran out by date** produces no Moodle event. Such a ticket
  lives to the end of its lifetime; the ticket lifetime is set by the Moodle
  administrator to the length of a session.
- **A video link forwarded outside the course** does not play from another
  address — that is a protection, not a fault.
