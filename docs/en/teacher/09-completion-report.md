**Reports → Activity completion** shows who opened the activity and who
collected the required minutes, next to the completion of assignments and
quizzes.

![The course completion report](../../img/guide/08-completion-report.png)

A reminder from section [Completion](04-completion.md): the minutes mark
appears after the player is closed plus the interval of the background task,
not at the moment of watching.
