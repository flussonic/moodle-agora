# The broadcast page

For: the learner.

Section topics: [Opening a broadcast](01-open.md) · [Badges and placeholders](02-badges.md) · **The broadcast page** · [The recording after the broadcast](04-recording.md) · [Why it may not play](05-not-playing.md) · [Where to see your own completion](06-completion.md)

---

The page is laid out in two rows.

**On top, the player, and to the right of it the list of the broadcasts of this
course**, as tall as the player. The open one is marked, the others carry a
state badge and a frame. The heading of the list shows the number of broadcasts
in the course and, when completion is on in the course, how many of them you
have completed. On a narrow screen the list is collapsed in height and the
"Show all" link unfolds it.

**Came before the start — just stay on the page.** While the player area says
"The broadcast has not started yet", the page checks every 15–20 seconds
whether the broadcast has started and, once it has, shows the player — no need
to reload. It takes no more than 35 seconds from the start of the broadcast to
the player.

![Before the start: "The broadcast has not started yet" in place of the player](../../img/guide/10-student-not-started.png)

![The same tab, not reloaded: the broadcast has started and the player replaced the placeholder](../../img/guide/10-student-auto-live.png)

If you are watching the recording of an earlier session at that moment, it is
not cut off: a "The broadcast has started." bar with a "Go to the broadcast"
button appears above the player. Press it and the recording gives way to the
broadcast; leave it and you finish the recording.

![Above the recording of an earlier session — the "The broadcast has started. Go to the broadcast" bar](../../img/guide/10-student-live-banner.png)

**Below, the comments**, and to the right of them the cards of your other
courses.

The comments are the ordinary Moodle ones: the new comment field on top, the
feed below it, posting without a page reload. Your teacher sees the same feed
and can delete someone else's comment. Comments are not tied to a moment in the
video — this is not a chat.

![A learner comment under the player](../../img/guide/06-student-comments.png)

**The cards of other courses** — up to six courses you are enrolled in, and
only those with at least one session available to you. A card leads not to the
course page but straight to a session:

1. if a broadcast is running in that course right now — to it; such a card
   carries the Live badge and comes first;
2. otherwise, when completion is counted in the course — to the first session
   with the "view the activity" condition that you have not opened yet; the
   subtitle then reads "Next: …". Sessions without that condition do not count:
   there is nowhere to see whether you opened them;
3. otherwise — to the first available session of the course. That also covers
   courses where completion is off, where no session carries the "view the
   activity" condition, and where you have already opened every such session.

If you have only one course and there is nothing to show, the course column is
not there at all — its neighbour takes the space and no empty frames are left.

On a narrow screen both right-hand columns move down, under the player.
