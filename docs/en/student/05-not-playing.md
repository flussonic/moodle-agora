# Why it may not play

For: the learner.

Section topics: [Opening a broadcast](01-open.md) · [Badges and placeholders](02-badges.md) · [The broadcast page](03-broadcast-page.md) · [The recording after the broadcast](04-recording.md) · **Why it may not play** · [Where to see your own completion](06-completion.md)

---

- **You are not enrolled in the course**, or you were unenrolled. The activity
  page then does not open at all — you get the enrolment page with a refusal
  instead. Talk to your teacher.
- **The broadcast has not happened yet**, or it went without a recording — look
  at the badge ([Badges and placeholders](02-badges.md)).
- **Your network changed** — you went from Wi-Fi to mobile internet, say.
  **Reload the activity page**: the access to the video will be issued anew.
- **You opened a link somebody forwarded to you.** A video link only works for
  the person it was issued to, and only from their address. That is a
  protection, not a fault: open the session from your own course.
- **The badge says "State unknown".** That is not on your side — tell your
  teacher.
