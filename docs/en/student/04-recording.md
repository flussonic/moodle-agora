# The recording after the broadcast

For: the learner.

Section topics: [Opening a broadcast](01-open.md) · [Badges and placeholders](02-badges.md) · [The broadcast page](03-broadcast-page.md) · **The recording after the broadcast** · [Why it may not play](05-not-playing.md) · [Where to see your own completion](06-completion.md)

---

If the session was recorded, the recording plays on the same page once the
broadcast is over — as a regular clip you can seek along. No second visit, no
other place, no separate link: the activity that held the broadcast shows
the recording too.

The clip does not appear instantly. The finished recording is first exported
into a clip on the Agora server — usually a matter of minutes; until the
export is ready, the player spot says "The recording will appear here
shortly.". An open page checks for readiness on its own and swaps the line
for the player — no need to reload it.

![The activity page: the broadcast is over, the recording is being prepared](../../img/guide/06-student-record.png)

A recording is kept for a limited time — set not by the course but by the
settings of the video server.

In activities created before teachers started running broadcasts themselves,
and in operator-run streams, the recording may play differently — as an
archive feed from the start to the end: seeking works, but there is no live
edge. It is the same player on the same page.

The line "This broadcast was not recorded" means exactly what it says: the
session went without a recording and there is no second viewing.

If a broadcast starts again on the same page — say, the next session runs
under the same activity — the recording you are watching is not cut off. A
"The broadcast has started." bar with a "Go to the broadcast" button appears
above the player: press it and the broadcast opens in place of the recording.
