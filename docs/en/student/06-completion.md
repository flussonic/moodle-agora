# Where to see your own completion

For: the learner.

Section topics: [Opening a broadcast](01-open.md) · [Badges and placeholders](02-badges.md) · [The broadcast page](03-broadcast-page.md) · [The recording after the broadcast](04-recording.md) · [Why it may not play](05-not-playing.md) · **Where to see your own completion**

---

If the teacher switched completion tracking on, the activity card shows what is
expected of you: open the session, or watch it for at least the stated number
of minutes. The minutes you have collected are shown there too.

The watching mark appears **not during the session**: the site counts the
minutes from the viewing history after you close the player and sets the mark
with a delay — usually up to ten minutes. If the mark is not there right away,
wait and refresh the course page.

The time counted runs from opening the player to its last request to the video
server. Once you close the tab, the server keeps your viewing open for about a
minute in case you come back; that minute is not counted.
