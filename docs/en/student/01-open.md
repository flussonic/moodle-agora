# Opening a broadcast

For: the learner.

Section topics: **Opening a broadcast** · [Badges and placeholders](02-badges.md) · [The broadcast page](03-broadcast-page.md) · [The recording after the broadcast](04-recording.md) · [Why it may not play](05-not-playing.md) · [Where to see your own completion](06-completion.md)

---

The session sits on the course page as an ordinary activity — next to
assignments and quizzes. It is called **Agora broadcast**. There is nothing to
install and nowhere else to log in: the video plays right on the page, under
your Moodle account.

![The course page through the learner's eyes](../../img/guide/06-student-course.png)

The activity card shows the name of the session, the state badge, a frame from
the broadcast and the start date, if the teacher set one.
